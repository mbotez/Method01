Delete Your Canonic Beauty Account

Company Name: Daybreak Technologies Ltd App Name: Canonic Beauty Contact Email: support@canonic.co.uk

Daybreak Technologies Ltd ("we," "our," or "us") provides options for users to delete their Canonic Beauty account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account in the App

You can delete your account directly inside the Canonic Beauty application by following these steps:

  • Open the Canonic Beauty app and navigate to your Profile.
  • Tap on Settings.
  • Select Delete Account.
  • Confirm your deletion by typing DELETE and tapping the final confirmation button.

How to Request Deletion Without the App

If you are unable to access the application or prefer to request account deletion externally, you can email support@canonic.co.uk from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account deletion request". We will process your request promptly.

What Data is Deleted

Upon successful completion of an account deletion request, the following data is permanently removed from our production databases:

  • Account Details: Email address, username, and sign-in credentials (including Google Auth linkage and password records).
  • Skin Questionnaire Answers: All chat questionnaire responses, skin type preferences, and lifestyle selections.
  • Tracker and Product Data: Skin logs, cosmetic product entries, custom ratings, and product feedback.
  • Follows and Social Data: Social connections, follows, posts, comments, and messages.
  • Uploaded Photos: All uploaded front-facing photographs and close-up skin images.

What is Kept and For How Long

We aim to complete account deletion and data removal requests within 30 days of receipt. Encrypted database backups containing historical snapshots are purged automatically within a further 30 days (60 days total from request).

Where specific transaction, audit, or tax records must be retained to comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations under UK law, minimal records are securely archived for the legally required retention period, after which they are permanently deleted. If no such legal obligations apply, no personal data is retained.
