Privacy Policy for Canonic Beauty
Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Company Name: Daybreak Technologies Ltd
Contact Email: support@canonic.co.uk
Daybreak Technologies Ltd ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Canonic Beauty mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and disclose personal data when you use our application.
We are committed to ensuring compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.
1. General Information Disclaimer
Canonic Beauty is an information display tool only and provides NO medical advice, clinical metrics, diagnoses, or treatments of any kind. The text inputs, chat questionnaire responses, and visual score outputs generated by this application are strictly for general informational display and cosmetic interest purposes. Nothing provided by this app constitutes professional medical, dermatological, or clinical guidance. Users should consult board-certified medical professionals or dermatologists for any skin conditions or health concerns.
2. Information We Collect
We collect information to provide and maintain our software features:
Account Information: When you log in using Google Authentication, we collect your account profile details, including your user name and email address.
Skin Questionnaire Data: We collect text inputs and selections you provide through our interactive chat questionnaire (such as skin type preferences, lifestyle habits, product filters, age group, and gender identity).
Photographic Data: If you choose to use our optional photo upload or camera feature, we temporarily process front-facing and close-up photographs to calculate software-generated cosmetic metric numbers to display back to you.
Automatically Collected Data: We log basic technical data to monitor traffic and fix bugs, such as IP addresses, browser types, and request timestamps.
3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data for the following operational software functions:
Service Delivery: To authenticate your identity via Google Auth and maintain your secure user profile and preferences.
Data Display: To process your questionnaire responses and photograph uploads so your personal selections and calculated cosmetic metrics can be rendered back to you on screen.
Platform Maintenance: To monitor performance and maintain the security and stability of our database infrastructure hosted on Supabase.
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or photographs to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
4. Data Retention & Your Rights
Retention: We retain your account data and profile history for as long as your account remains active. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting us at support@canonic.co.uk, upon which your personally identifiable information will be promptly removed.
Your GDPR Rights: Under UK data protection law, you have the right to access, update, correct, or request the deletion of the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent at any time.
5. Security
We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to secure your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss, utilizing encrypted connections and secure infrastructure via Supabase. However, no security system is entirely impenetrable.
6. Teen Users
Our service is available to users of all ages, including teens. If you are under the age of 18, you confirm that you have reviewed this policy with a parent or legal guardian who understands and consents to your use of the application and the processing of your data.
7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal changes. We will post the revised policy within our application or digital properties.
